   normal year-to-year range — robust z of −1.4, and we only flag beyond ±3.5."
3. **The latest season**: "The answer is the same whether the season runs October to April, November to

4. **See all seasons**: "One button opens every season with its 95% range from resampled days."
5. **Counting or measuring intensity**: "We check MODIS against VIIRS on fire radiative power, at the same
