How it works

1. Each season total is compared with the other seasons using a robust z-score: distance to the median divided by 1.4826 × the median absolute deviation; flagged only beyond ±3.5, a threshold fixed in advance.

2. The 95% range resamples the days of each month (bootstrap), because detections cluster in time far more than a Poisson count would.

3. Sensors are compared on fire radiative power, an index of fire intensity, during daytime passes at the same hour (Suomi NPP vs Aqua) near the centre of the swath, where pixels are smallest.

Words used
MODIS, VIIRS, Suomi NPP, Aqua, Terra, NOAA-20
NASA and NOAA fire sensors and satellites. MODIS (on Terra and Aqua) has 1 km pixels; VIIRS (on Suomi NPP, NOAA-20, NOAA-21) has 375 m pixels.
FRP (fire radiative power)
The heat a fire radiates at the moment of the satellite pass, in megawatts (MW). Summed over detections, it is an index of fire intensity.
Robust z-score
How unusual a value is, measured with the median and the median absolute deviation, so that one extreme year does not hide another.
Provisional
Near real-time data that NASA may still revise.
