Cameroon fire calendar

NASA's two fire sensors, MODIS and VIIRS, count fires differently; this calendar puts them on one scale so you can see, month by month since 2012, whether Cameroon had more or fewer fires than usual.

The problem

MODIS and VIIRS do not see fires the same way: VIIRS has smaller pixels and records about four times more fire detections, so their counts cannot be compared directly. Anyone who wants to know whether a month is unusually active has no single, consistent series to read. This calendar uses one sensor as the reference (Suomi NPP, VIIRS) and rescales MODIS year by year, so every month of Cameroon since 2012 is on the same scale.

Rescaling MODIS, year by year

How many Suomi NPP detections correspond to one MODIS detection, computed each year over the months both sensors fully cover (nominal and high confidence). It changes over time, so it is never a single constant.

Cross-check with NOAA-20 and NOAA-21

These two VIIRS satellites often see the same fires as Suomi NPP, so they are not added together; they only check that the main series moves the same way.

Words used

Fire detection (hotspot)
A satellite pixel that was hotter than its surroundings when the satellite passed. One large fire can give several detections; a small fire under clouds gives none. Detections are not burned area.
Usual level (median)
The middle value of that calendar month over 2012-2025.
Compared with usual (anomaly)
(detections this month − usual level) / usual level × 100.
MODIS, VIIRS, Suomi NPP
NASA fire sensors. MODIS (Terra, Aqua) since 2000, 1 km pixels; VIIRS since 2012, 375 m pixels, on Suomi NPP (the main series here), NOAA-20 and NOAA-21.
Provisional
Near real-time data, or one of the last five months: may change when NASA publishes the final data.
FRP (fire radiative power)
The heat a fire gives off, in megawatts. Not shown on this page; compared between sensors on the development branch.
