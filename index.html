Cameroon fires, month by month

Satellite fire hotspots, 2012 to 2026. Main series: VIIRS S-NPP; MODIS is harmonized with a calibration factor computed for each year. Color: how each month compares with its historical median.

Provisional data: hatched months () come from near real-time (NRT) data or are among the last five months; they may still change. 2026 is incomplete.
MODIS gap: no MODIS data from . August and September 2026 are incomplete for MODIS (the main S-NPP series is not affected).

S-NPP / MODIS calibration, by year

S-NPP detections per MODIS detection, over months complete for both sensors (nominal and high confidence). Never a single constant.

Cross-check with NOAA-20 and NOAA-21

The sensors often see the same fire, so they are not added together. NOAA-20 and NOAA-21 are used only to check the S-NPP series over the reference months.

Data: NASA FIRMS (Fire Information for Resource Management System), MODIS Collection 6.1 and VIIRS 375 m (S-NPP, NOAA-20, NOAA-21), firms.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov.
